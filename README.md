# Marrow & Vellum

Marrow & Vellum is the workshop repository.

This archive holds ideas, prototypes, experiments, technical fragments, utilities, and concepts that are not yet mature enough to become dedicated projects.

Projects may later graduate into their own repositories.

## Purpose

Marrow & Vellum answers:

- What ideas are being explored?
- What experiments are worth testing?
- What concepts are incubating?
- What deserves future investment?

This is the workshop, not the cathedral.
